# Datei Ma Kuromi

Ally 1: Kagemori's elder sister and representative (hostage) in the capital. She was named such as the ruling family's most distinguished child and the one expected to rule in the future and has made the role her life's work. The declaration that her younger brother would rule the clan instead came as a surprise to many, but apparently not her. Then again, to hide veils behind veils is the Datei way.

## Notable Actions/Mechanics
(Dice pools for allied, non-combatant NPCs are largely decorative and for intuitive reference, unless a particular scene calls for rolls on their part.)

- **Courtly Debate:** 7 dice
- **Eavesdropping:** 8 dice
- **Gossip:** 6 dice
- **Poetry:** 7 dice
- **Read Intentions:** 9 dice
- **Lip Reading:** Kuromi is capable of eavesdropping on conversations out of earshot if she has a good view of the interlocutors' mouths.
- **Social Spider:** When Kuromi learns about a major or defining intimacy of another character second hand, she can attempt to introduce or challenge a fact about that person's relationships with her Read Intentions pool.

# Nazubi Gentle Shroud

Ally 3: the clan deity. Daimyo of summer rains and witness to secret contracts; patron of spies, affairs, and conspiracies. He covers his face with a thin, damp shroud at all times and to swear him to secrecy is to reveal yourself to him—but he is a trustworthy keeper of secrets.

When Clan Datei was great, his domain was the sweet summer rain which cools the air and speeds travel, the sunshower which blesses unions, the light fog which lends privacy to meetings. In their exile, his rains hide the sound of footsteps and the watchful eyes of his kin.

## Notable actions/mechanics
(Dice pools for allied, non-combatant NPCs are largely decorative and for intuitive reference, unless a particular scene calls for rolls on their part.)

- **Weather Prediction:** 6 dice (8 dice in summer)
- **Stealth:** 9 dice
- **Knowledge of the 99 Stars' Affairs:** 7 dice
- **Perfectly Timed Distractions:** 8 dice
- **Measure the Wind:** Nazubi Gentle Shroud may take the measure of anyone who swears him to secrecy.
- **Voices Lost in Rain:** Nazubi treats social influence attempting to make him divulge information he's agreed to keep secret as unacceptable.
