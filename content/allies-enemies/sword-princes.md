# The First and Hundredth

The enigmatic patron god of the imperial clan, who bestowed the Imperial Daiklave upon the first Sword Prince, and leader of the Ninety-Nine Stars as the Sword leads the clan of Vaneha. Vaneha's foremost (though not only) god of war, they rarely appear in public, and when they do their countenance is shrouded from mortal eyes. Their priests typically wear tiger pelts, or tiger-patterned cloth, mirroring the tiger-and-centipede iconography of Vaneha's military banners.
