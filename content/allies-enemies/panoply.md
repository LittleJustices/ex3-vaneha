# Sakamori's Armor
**White Jade Breastplate, Artifact 3**

A suit of armor worn by Datei ma Sakamori during the Datei-Iachi war, which the circle discovered in his resting place at the footstep shrine.

The helmet isn't present—perhaps Ozane took it along with Sakamori's head. The arm and leg guards, greaves and gauntlets are less pristine than the breastplate, though still well preserved. The breastplate, though, is spotless, if dusty. There's a white sheen to it that River Snake recognizes after some inspection as an alloy of white jade and steel, padded with leather from a beast none of you recognize, though hardly any ordinary cow.

The Celestial Lion Ka Handu has issued a letter of recommendation addressed to an armorer in Great Forks, who should be capable of forging additional plates to replace the arm and leg guards of plain steel that have been added, which would upgrade the suit to Medium armor.

**Attunement:** 4m  
**Type:** Light (+5 Soak, Hardness 4, Mobility Penalty −0)  
**Tags:** None  
**Hearthstone slot(s):** 1  
**Era:** War of Veil and Vine  
## Datei War-Mask

> [!info]- Visual inspiration
> ![[ayakashi.jpg]]  
> 
> http://www.sense-nohgaku.com/noh/articles/syozoku/men_info/ayakashi.php

"Gifted" to Kagemori by Clan Anori as a token of appreciation for the Circle's discreet rescue of the Anori hostages. It depicts a warrior in their final moments, snarling at death. The mask is old but exquisitely wrought and hardly worn. Its color reminds of yellowed ivory, but it's cool to the touch, as if it were in fact made of stone. The warrior's pointed eyebrows and mustache, swept fiercely outward, are made of many individual threads of shiny grey metal inlaid into the mask. They glitter like a prism as they catch the light and resonate with something at the touch.

It gives its wearer two abilities while attuned:
- When a warrior under their command or otherwise fighting for their sake dies, the wearer can learn the deceased's most dearly held defining intimacy as if through an automatically successful reflexive read intentions action, if the dead warrior was significant enough in the story to have their intimacies represented individually.
- The wearer can make a special (dicepool to be determined) roll to introduce facts involving the defining or major intimacies of warriors who have died fighting for them or under their command.

The mask was originally part of Sakamori's armor, meant to fit into the missing helmet. It costs one mote to attune by itself, but when worn together with the armor, both its artifact rating and attunement cost are subsumed into the armor's.

# Treasures of the Suzugion

## Audient Brushes
Artifact 2

A pair of writing brushes with shafts of jade and brush-hairs of shining silver. One is decorated with blue jade and ivory in a spiral pattern, the other is red jade with onyx insets. When attuned for a mote, they count as exceptional equipment and produce their own ink in any color the scribe pleases. For three motes, a brush can be set to transcribe speech in its immediate vicinity and does so with the skill of a professional scribe.

See Arms of the Chosen, pp. 115f for details.

## Crystal of Protection
Artifact 3

A fist-sized crystal statuette carved in the likeness of Dendan, imbued with an obscure form of magic that to modern eyes is largely indistinguishable from First Age artifice.

Committing 5 motes of essence to the crystal causes it to project a dome of softly glowing sunfire that protects the area within, about the size of a single spacious room, from the elements and intruders, burning any who try to enter with an environmental hazard. The dome remains for a full day or until anyone inside leaves, at which point the statuette must be exposed to sunlight for at least half a day before it can function again.

Adapted from the Dragon King artifact of the same name in Ruins of Rathess, p. 86.

## Dreamstones
Resources 3 apiece

A half-dozen acorn-sized green opals archived in the Suzugion's library, each capable of holding a dream inside. Four of them are currently occupied, implying that the dreams inside them must be from the First Age. The remaining two are empty.

See Arms of the Chosen, p. 114 for details.

## Furniture
Resources 4–5

Chairs and sedans that once outfitted the Suzugion's study areas. Meant for scholars in their time, their materials and design would earn a prince's envy in the Age of Sorrows.

All together, those items the circle liberated can be liquidated for the equivalent of a Resources 5 purchase; split up, they make several fine Resources 4 gifts.

## Literature

Even after the Sidereals confiscated many tomes, the contents of the Suzugion's library could found several universities. A selection of documents from there makes a fine gift for scholars and princes, or can be kept as exceptional equipment for Lore and Occult rolls regarding astrology and astronomy and related sciences. It also allows a reader who takes the time to consult it to make Lore rolls as if they had a background in this field.
## Relief of the Hunters

An ancient and exquisitely carved stone relief depicting Cometborn Zhang with his daughters standing victorious over the legendary fish Dendan. A picture of a happier time, it is mundane but was received with gratitude by Kamo and Moka as a memento.

## Winterbreath Jars
Artifact 2

A pair of standing amphorae always filled with a chilly air that preserves foodstuffs placed within (or anything else one cares to preserve). Each holds about three gallons of liquid or a large melon or haunch of meat.

See Arms of the Chosen, p. 118 for details.

## Oadenol's Wheel
Artifact 3

A single large wheel with a glint of starmetal along its rim. It can be wielded as a weapon by an attuned wielder, but more importantly, its wielder can summon the rest of a chariot forth from it as a miscellaneous action.

The chariot is an elegant sweep of dark wood with a glint of starmetal and a light frosting of ice along the spokes of its wheels, drawn by the spirit-mares Skere and Adrath. The chariot has room for its driver and up to two passengers. Riding a chariot in combat uses the mounted combat rules for the steeds drawing the chariot, but also provides light cover without requiring cover rolls. Passengers also gain the cover and mounted combat benefits. Skere and Adrath use horse traits, but add +2 to all dice pools and have soak 10 and Hardness 5. They're naturally material like elementals, and reform by the next sunrise or sunset if killed.

**Attunement:** 5m
**Type:** Heavy (+1 ACC, +14 DMG, +0 DEF, OVW 5)
**Tags:** Bashing, Brawl, Smashing, Thrown (Short), Two-Handed.
**Hearthstone Slots:** 2
**Era:** Labors of the Tekhelet Circle

See Sidereals, pp. 378ff for details and possible evocations, though these are written with Sidereals in mind.
## Talion
Artifact 4

An orichalcum staff forged from the righteous indignation of Old Man Kai, who drew his second breath to berate the Sun's own high priest for pride and callousness.

An attuned wielder may declare a grievance against someone who has offended against one of their major or defining intimacies. This grievance becomes a new defining intimacy until it is redressed and provides bonuses to track down and pursue the offender.

**Attunement:** 5m
**Type:** Medium (+3 ACC, +12 DMG, +1 DEF, OVW 4)
**Tags:** Bashing, Melee, Reaching
**Hearthstone slot(s):** 1
**Era:** Era of Limitless Light

See Arms of the Chosen, pp. 74ff for details and possible evocations.
## The Heaven and Earth Gauntlets
Artifact 3

A pair of heavy fighting gauntlets composed of articulated orichalcum plates, engraved with characters for heaven and earth. They feel warm to the touch. Inside the padding is an embroidered dedication, exhorting someone named Ajni to fight for justice and be safe. According to the Chosen of Secrets Marea, a mentor of hers made them for her brother, very long ago. The mentor felt one of you might be meant to have them.

The Heaven and Earth Gauntlets’ clash attack bonus when wielded paired rises to (the higher of Essence or 3).

**Attunement:** 5m
**Type:** Light (+5 ACC, +10 DMG, +0 DEF, OVW 3)
**Tags:** Bashing, Brawl, Grappling, Smashing, Worn
**Hearthstone slot(s):** 2
**Era:** Broken Blade Concordat

See Arms of the Chosen, pp. 35ff for details and possible evocations.
## Scroll of the Difficult Choice

This beautifully illustrated scroll's pictures tell of the splendor of god-kings, then hubris and cruelty, a meeting of abused advisors and retainers, divinations and debates, and a difficult decision to overthrow the tyrants who threatened to burn the world itself. Would you rather have a lesser world, or a dead one, the final images seem to ask.
